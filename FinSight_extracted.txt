Product Requirements Document · Version 1.0 · October 2026
1. Product overview
FinSight is a voice-first personal financial intelligence platform that transforms fragmented financial transaction records into actionable insights through multilingual voice conversations, conversational analytics, anomaly detection and predictive financial intelligence.
Users can upload their bank statements in CSV format, analyse their UPI and other transactions, understand spending patterns, forecast cash flows and ask questions about their finances through natural conversations.
Gnani AI provides the core speech capabilities, while FinSight's own analytics and AI layers generate financial intelligence.
Product vision: Make personal financial management conversational, intelligent, predictive and accessible.
2. Problem statement
People increasingly use UPI, debit cards, bank transfers and other digital payment methods. However, their financial information is often fragmented and difficult to interpret.
The major problems are:
Financial transactions are scattered across multiple accounts and payment platforms.
Users lack visibility into where their money is actually going.
Conventional expense trackers display spending but rarely explain why it changes.
Users identify overspending only after their money has been spent.
Most applications provide limited predictive cash-flow intelligence.
Financial dashboards can be difficult to navigate and understand.
Existing financial tools provide limited natural-language and multilingual interaction.
Our solution: A unified conversational financial analyst that helps users understand historical transactions, investigate financial behaviour and anticipate future financial conditions.

User query / action
FinSight response

Produces a visual report and a concise conversational summary.
Flagship demonstration scenario
A user uploads three months of bank statements and asks:
"Why did my expenses increase this month?"
FinSight:
Retrieves and analyses the relevant transactions.
Compares current and previous monthly spending.
Identifies categories responsible for the increase.
Detects unusual transactions where appropriate.
Generates a visual breakdown.
Explains the findings using Gnani's speech generation.
This will be our primary hackathon demo.

10. Success metrics and evaluation
We need to measure actual product performance, not just the number of AI features.
Metric
Proposed target
Transaction categorization accuracy
≥90% on labelled test data
Financial calculation correctness
≥99% on deterministic test cases
Voice query task success
≥90% on supported test queries
Analytical answer grounding
≥95% on a curated evaluation set
Anomaly detection precision
≥75% on labelled synthetic anomalies
CSV ingestion success
≥95% on supported statement formats
Forecasting performance
Beat a seasonal-naive baseline on test data
End-to-end query latency
Target under 8 seconds for non-streaming analytical responses
These are proposed engineering acceptance targets, not existing performance claims. Forecasting and anomaly metrics must be evaluated on held-out data, and voice latency should be measured separately from the complete analytical workflow.
